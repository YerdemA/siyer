The Life of the Prophet tells the life of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) chapter by chapter, through animated illustrations and narration. Each chapter is like a short documentary built from layered, moving scenes, and every sentence in it is backed by a source.

FIRST PERIOD: BEFORE PROPHETHOOD
Ten chapters, 135 scenes, about 42 minutes of narration: pre-Islamic Mecca, the Year of the Elephant, his birth, the years with his foster mother Halima, orphanhood, the caravan to Syria with Abu Talib, youth and the Hilf al-Fudul pact, al-Amin and his marriage to Khadija, the rebuilding of the Ka'ba, and the retreats to the cave of Hira.

The period of prophethood is on its way: new chapters will appear in the app as they are completed.

BASED ON SOURCES
Every fact is linked to a source: the Qur'an, the Sahih collections of al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Hisham's Sira and the TDV Encyclopedia of Islam. Each chapter ends with its list of sources, with work and page; the full bibliography is in Settings. Where reports differ, the strongest one is told and the others are noted. Quoted verses in English follow Pickthall's translation (1930).

RESPECTFUL AND FOR ALL AGES
Plain, respectful language. The Prophet is shown only as light, without face or body details; other prophets are never drawn as figures.

FEATURES
• 10 narrated chapters of animated scenes (narration in Turkish)
• English and Turkish subtitles; app language English or Turkish
• Illustrated map from Mecca to Syria
• Timeline of events and a glossary of 50 people
• Subtitle size, 1.5× and 2× speed, scene skipping
• Lock-screen controls: listen with the screen off
• Resume where you left off; progress stays on your device

NO ADS, NO ACCOUNT, OFFLINE
Completely free. No ads, no account, no data collection. The first chapter comes with the app; the others download once when you open them (3–10 MB) and then play offline.
